How it works

One trusted source of PQC rules, kept current for you.

Standards change. New documents appear, drafts become final, deadlines move. The Standards Manager tracks them so your assessments don't silently go out of date.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Sources such as NIST and the NSA are checked for new and updated publications. Changes are fetched, archived and compared with what you have.

  2. 2

    Review

    Nothing changes without review. Each proposed rule is checked against its source passage and approved by a person, with a recorded reason.

  3. 3

    Release

    Approved changes go into a numbered release with public release notes. Any past assessment can be re-run against the release it used.

  4. 4

    Assess

    Your tools call the API, one crypto use or thousands at once, and get verdicts, deadlines, replacements and the cited rules back.

What you get

Answers your assessment can stand behind.

Verdicts with dates

Approved, deprecated or disallowed today, plus the date each status changes and the next transition deadline for your kind of system.

What to move to

Approved replacements for each use: ML-KEM for key exchange, ML-DSA or SLH-DSA for signatures, stronger sizes where that is enough.

Right for the system

Ordinary business systems, US national security systems, federal high-value assets or FIPS-only environments: each gets the rules and deadlines that apply to it.

Your standards, your scope

Assess against all approved standards, or only the ones your regulator or policy names. Set the list once for a whole batch.

Evidence for auditors

Every verdict lists the rules behind it, with the standard, section and date, linked to the official publication.

Continuously checked

The service is scanned for known vulnerabilities weekly and on every change, and its full test suite runs on a freshly built database each time.
