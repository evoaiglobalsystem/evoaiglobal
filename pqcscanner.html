Encrypted traffic recorded today can be read later.

Attackers can store encrypted traffic now and decrypt it once quantum computers are powerful enough. Hybrid ML-KEM key exchange, standardised by NIST as FIPS 203, closes that gap, and many sites already support it. The question is whether yours does, on every address you serve.

Common questions

How do I get access?

The scanner uses API keys issued by evoAI. Contact us to request one, then enter it in the scanner dashboard or send it in the X-API-Key header when using the API.

Why is the best score 79 and not 100?

Full marks on certificate checks need a post-quantum certificate, and publicly trusted CAs do not issue them yet. The report shows the score against what is achievable today.

Is this a compliance audit?

No. It is an informational technical assessment of what your servers offer at scan time. It does not certify compliance with any standard.

Can I scan any website?

Only systems you own or are authorised to test.

Will the score change over time?

Standards and browser support are moving quickly, so the scoring rules are reviewed regularly. Each report states the scanner version and when the standards data was last reviewed.
